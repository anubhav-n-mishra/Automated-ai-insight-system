    How it works
    
          
      
            
    1. Profile. Column roles and a date range are inferred locally from the file you upload.
      2. 
            
    2. Aggregate. Each metric is reduced with its own aggregation; ratios are computed after aggregation, never averaged across rows.
      3. 
            
    3. Rank. Segments are scored by their share of the total movement, weighted by KPI priority and damped by materiality.
      4. 
            
    4. Attribute. Each total movement is decomposed across the segments that caused it, with the unexplained remainder stated.
      5. 
            
    5. Render. A deck, a tokenised dashboard and an audio briefing, all from the same numbers.
      6. 
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Good to know
    
          
      
            
    • 
              Percentages against zero.
              
      A movement from a zero baseline is reported as n/a, not 100%.
      
            
      • 
            
    • 
              Small segments.
              
      Segments below 0.1% of a metric are excluded, so a three-row segment cannot take the headline with a 900% swing.
      
            
      • 
            
    • 
              Narrative is optional.
              
      With no model provider configured, the summary is written deterministically from the computed numbers.
      
            
      • 
            
    • 
              Share links expire.
              
      Dashboard links carry a single-use-style token and stop working after 72 hours by default.
